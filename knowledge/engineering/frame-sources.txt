AURA frame and designer workflow research — 2026-10-22

Implemented scope
Original AURA construction.js generates actual raked rear posts and lower/mid/upper
rails for sofa/loveseat/armchair, mounts reaching real leg axes or sled tube ends,
and concealed floor glides when a skirt/zero-leg concept has no structural feet.
Existing seat stretchers now reach the perimeter rails. Frame-base rear timber is
visible; internal members are visible in the studio/designer structure view and BOM.
This is original assembly code, not imported/certified Opendesk furniture.

World mesh bounding boxes form a2mm-tolerance POSSIBLE-contact graph, starting from
floor contacts. It detects removed/moved/broken members. Bounding-box contact is a
broad-phase approximation; it can overestimate contact and cannot establish joint
stiffness, valid fastening, moment transfer, fiber direction or assembly capacity.

Actual rectangular horizontal member dimensions also feed isolated simply-supported
beam scenarios: central1000N; assumed longitudinal E=9000MPa; I=b*h^3/12;
delta=P*L^3/(48*E*I); sigma=(P*L/4)*(h/2)/I. These are declared example assumptions,
not normative furniture loads or measured material properties.24 actual generated
members were independently checked against native PyNite displacement and reaction.
The complete sofa/table assembly was NOT solved by FEA or tested in a laboratory.

1. Purdue University, Carl A. Eckelman and Yusuf Z. Erdil, FNR-170,
Joint Design Manual for Furniture Frames Constructed of Plywood and Oriented Strand
Board: https://extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/FNR/FNR-170.pdf
35-page scanned PDF downloaded completely; pages1-5 visually read including Tables1-3.
The material/joint-dependent nature of strength, workmanship variability and the
distinction between cyclic and static conditions are relevant. Historical GSA loads
were NOT imported as current worldwide limits. No capacity formula for a particular
plywood/dowel/glue was transferred to unidentified solid-wood joints in AURA. Remaining
pages and joint geometries remain to be reviewed; no complete-source-review claim.
First host reset, next transfer timed out at6307840bytes, official alternate host

Workflow
Customer assessment dialog is shown before POST, includes readable assessment.txt
and original designs/reports in assessment.json ZIP. A changed item/quantity requires
fresh confirmation; disabled backend never sends. Download is allowed while backend
is unavailable. Original contact data is not included in this assessment download.
Designer auto-correct produces a separate browser-local revision, keeps original,
supports restore/compare and persists later edits separately from customer project.
Known proposals: restore removed generated frame parts; increase seating leg radius
within existing limits until assumed D24 scenario is met; wooden-only legs-only table
gets60mm-high/25mm-thick concept aprons. These are proposals, not normative dimensions.
Custom geometry/transform/cut overrides are never discarded to fabricate a pass.
Unknown material grading, real joints, all-age fit, fatigue/impact/tipping, aesthetics
remain explicit. All reports keep production_approved=false; no all-issues-fixed claim.
Legacy physics “all checks passed” summary is suppressed in designer view; its other
outputs are visibly labeled assumed scenarios. Previous PR220 material fix remains
separate and must be preserved on integration. No merge/deploy/production submission.
