# Furniture drawing research archive

The full 87-type corpus remains in `w4/assets/furniture-taxonomy.js` (PRODUCTS), `reference-sources.js`, and `reference-studies.js`. The public selection uses MAIN_PRODUCT_IDS only. Keep archived variants out of customer option lists.

The private visual comparison book and research images are retained in the workspace outputs/work folders, not deployed. This corpus is a reference for future drawing-tool evaluation and training preparation; no model has been trained on it. Check source-image rights and production geometry before assembling any training dataset.
