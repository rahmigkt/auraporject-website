WORLD AEROSPACE CONGRESS · BRAND IDENTITY

One mark.
A lasting identity.

An independent design system for consistent digital, print and spatial applications of the World Aerospace Congress identity.

01 / LOGOTYPE

WAC is a shared geometry of folded surfaces.

The W, A and C letterforms are formed from folded surfaces using identical ribbon widths and parallel diagonal angles. Light and shade represent paper folds rather than separate decorative objects. The master vector preserves the proportions of all three letters.

The folded WAC wordmark is the core identity element. SVG lockups are prepared for digital use; print applications should convert text to vector outlines and verify optical alignment.
01B / GEOMETRY

Measured folds and aligned planes.

The diagonal structural bands use 76-unit horizontal-cap widths and parallel diagonals with a 153-unit horizontal shift over 300 units of height. Fold shading indicates direction without changing the measured outlines.

At small sizes, fold shadows must not compete with the legibility of the letterforms.

02 / CONGRESS YEAR SYSTEM

20 over 27. Never a slash.

In the logo, the year is stacked vertically as 20 above 27 next to a fine divider. In ordinary copy, write the year as 2027. WAC remains the permanent institution; 2027 identifies the congress year.

03 / COLOUR

Colour with purpose.

Slate and graphite establish a lasting institutional identity. Mineral blue is used sparingly for structure and navigation; warm paper tones preserve clarity and calm. The wordmark uses tonal planes of one primary colour.

04 / TYPE

Precision before decoration.

The website uses a local system sans-serif stack without external font servers. Display weights are approximately 700; editorial copy is 400–500; technical labels 650–700.

06 / RULES

The identity remains coherent.

  • Do not separate the WAC fold layers, change their angles or distort individual ribbons.
  • Stack 20 and 27 vertically in identity lockups; use 2027 in sentences.
  • Show the Turkish Society for History of Science as text when institutional attribution is required. Its emblem is not part of the WAC identity.
  • Preserve the five official symposium titles in both languages.
  • Use imagery only when it adds meaning; do not compromise readability or contrast.
  • Keep the scientific symposium, forum, industry expo, delegations and optional cultural programme distinguishable.
