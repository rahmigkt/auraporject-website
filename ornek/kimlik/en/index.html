WORLD AEROSPACE CONGRESS · IDENTITY SYSTEM / V1.0

One mark.
A lasting identity.

An independent design system for consistent digital, print and spatial applications of the World Aerospace Congress identity.

01 / LOGOTYPE

The modular W is the constant.

The primary symbol is a geometric W constructed from three sharp modules. The existing approved vector must be reused without re-drawing or altering its proportions.

The modular symbol comes from the chosen concept and its current vector reconstruction. The horizontal lockups are editable SVG working versions; final print usage requires typographic and optical checks.
02 / EDITION SYSTEM

20 over 27. Never a slash.

In the logo, the year is stacked vertically as 20 above 27 next to a fine divider. In ordinary copy, write the year as 2027. WAC remains the permanent institution; 2027 identifies the edition.

03 / COLOUR

Colour with purpose.

Midnight navy#07121E

Ink#17212B

Paper#F7F7F5

Technical blue#2368BA

Dark navy and off-white form the principal identity. Blue is reserved for selected technical illustrations and functional accents. The logo must remain strong in single colour.

04 / TYPE

Precision before decoration.

The website uses a local system sans-serif stack without external font servers. Display weights are approximately 700; editorial copy is 400–500; technical labels 650–700.

Long titles, language-specific line breaks, readable content width and keyboard navigation must be considered in every implementation.

05 / APPLICATIONS

One system, many formats.

Compact mark

Icons, screen corners and monochrome applications.

Dark application

Stage screens, motion graphics and digital presentations.

06 / RULES

The identity remains coherent.

  • Do not reassemble, stretch, distort or recolour individual W modules.
  • Stack 20 and 27 vertically in identity lockups; use 2027 in sentences.
  • Show the Turkish Society for History of Science as text when institutional attribution is required. Its emblem is not part of the WAC identity.
  • Preserve the five official symposium titles in both languages.
  • Use imagery only when it adds meaning; do not compromise readability or contrast.
  • Keep the scientific symposium, forum, industry expo, delegations and optional cultural programme distinguishable.
