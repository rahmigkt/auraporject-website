07 — INTERNATIONAL HOSTING

How future host countries are selected.

The proposed nomination, evaluation and voting process from the fourth congress onward.

Application process ↗
2036+International hosts
01 / SCOPE

The first three congresses in Türkiye, followed by international nominations.

The drafts envisage the first three congresses in Türkiye. From the fourth congress, a hosting nomination may be submitted only by a recognised national Delegation.

2027

Istanbul

Founding congress, planned for 27–29 October.

2030

Türkiye

Second congress; expanding the international network.

2033

Istanbul ambition

Third congress; the city is subject to the competent board's decision.

2036+

International

Selection of host countries through delegation candidacies.

02 / CANDIDACY

What a hosting bid must include.

01

Timing

Written application at least six months before the relevant election.

02

Venue and facilities

Conference rooms, technology systems and expo capacity.

03

Accessibility

International transport and accommodation.

04

Financial capacity

Resources and anticipated support.

05

Safety and dates

Health and security provisions and proposed event dates.

03 / SELECTION AND TRANSFER

From evaluation to local delivery.

01

Initial evaluation

The competent board reviews facilities, finance, access and safety.

02

Presentation and vote

Candidate countries present; a simple majority of delegations is required.

03

Hosting agreement

Transfer protocol between the selected delegation and Academic Coordination Institution.

04

Organising Committee

The host establishes a local committee responsible for its own finances.

Draft rules

The information derives from the draft Host-Country Regulation. Certain board and governance terms differ between draft charters and require confirmation.
