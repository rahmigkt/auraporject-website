SCIENTIFIC PROGRAMME · FIVE SYMPOSIA

Five official symposia. One academic framework.

The official abstract categories and scientific review framework are presented here.

02— 05

01 / OFFICIAL TITLES

Official symposium categories

01

Aerospace Sciences and Engineering

Flight sciences, aerodynamics, engineering, aircraft and systems research.

02

Space Sciences and Technologies

Satellite systems, space engineering, mission technologies and research.

03

Aviation Management, Economics and Law

Management, operations, economics, finance, regulation and legal aspects of aviation.

04

Aerospace Ecosystem

Institutions, technologies, production and research relationships across aerospace.

05

Aviation History and Society

Scientific history of flight and space, archives, heritage and social impact.

02 / REVIEW

Scientific review

These are the five official symposium categories defined in the draft Congress Implementation Regulation. The Scientific Committee may identify sub-themes while retaining these primary titles.

The draft regulation envisages at least two reviewers appointed by the Scientific Committee for each submission, with accepted works included in the congress proceedings.

Call for abstracts

31 March 2027

Abstracts are submitted through the existing official submission platform. The announced deadline is 31 March 2027.
