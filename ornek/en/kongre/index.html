ISTANBUL · 27–29 OCTOBER 2027

One meeting. A lasting beginning.

Istanbul 2027 is planned as the founding congress of an international platform intended to sustain scientific and institutional work beyond a single event.

01— 05
PLANNED DATES27–29 October 2027
LOCATIONIstanbul, Türkiye
CYCLEEvery three years

01 / WAC

The founding congress

WAC is envisaged as a triennial international platform covering aerospace sciences alongside engineering, management, economics, law and history. The planned dates for the founding congress are 27–29 October 2027.

02 / SCOPE

Wider scope

01Civil aviation
02Military aviation and defence technologies
03Space
Scientific categoriesThe wider congress scope comprises civil aviation, military aviation and defence technologies, and space. These three fields do not replace the five official symposium categories used for abstract submissions.
