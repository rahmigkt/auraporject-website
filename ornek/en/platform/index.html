01 / ELIGIBILITY

Who may apply for delegation status?

An applicant may be a legally established aviation- or space-related association, foundation, agency, professional body or relevant authority in its country. Representative capacity and adherence to WAC principles are assessed.

03 / REPRESENTATION

Representation rights

The Delegation Office makes an initial assessment and submits eligible applications to the Executive Board. Accepted organisations gain country-delegation status; consortia are encouraged when several bodies apply from one country.

Accepted delegations may be represented and vote in the General Assembly, take part in host selection, and propose experts and sessions. The delegation application form is not yet open.

04 / PRINCIPLES

Founding principles and academic coordination

The draft Founding Declaration sets out universality, independence, scientific integrity, cooperation, continuity, transparency and sustainability. Academic coordination is entrusted to the Turkish Society for History of Science (TBTK, 1989).
